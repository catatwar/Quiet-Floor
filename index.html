    Halvorsen Acoustic Annex · Chamber B

    The Quiet Floor

    Contains jumpscares, sudden loud sounds and brief flashing images.

    How to play

    Chamber B is the quietest room in Minnesota. In 1979 Ruth Ambler spent 31 hours inside it and came out unable to speak. Tonight you are sent in to re-certify it. The door seals behind you.

    GET OUT

    1. Take three noise-floor readings at the meter stands with the red lamps.
    2. When the door is gone, hold Q (TAPE on touch) to play back what the tape heard. It shows the real way out.
    3. Walk into the wedges where the tape shows the open door and hold E to push through.

    The objective list (top-left) and the amber marker always show what to do next. Logbook pages tell Ruth’s story. Spare reels add tape.

    THE TAPE IS YOUR LIFE

    It runs down slowly and much faster while you play it back. If Ruth reaches you, it skips and you get a breather while she loses you. At 00:00, the next time she reaches you, she keeps you.

    RUTHShe moves only when nothing is looking at her. She comes in waves: the lights stutter and the air goes cold. When red creeps in from one side of your view, she is there. Turn and put your light on her.
    THE ROOMIt is not always honest. Walls move. Things are written that weren’t there. If something seems wrong, play back the tape.
